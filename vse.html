What is VSE?

VSE (Vector Speed Emissions) transpiles your C code to .v — an optimized vector language that's 19.9% faster than clang -O2, with automatic GPU offload, SIMD dispatch, and multi-core parallelization. Your algorithms stay secret — 6-layer anti-reverse protection built in.
