## 1. What We Collect

### 1.1 Freemium (Free) Users
- **Hardware ID** (SHA-256 hash of device hostname + CPU + MAC + architecture) — used for license verification only
- **License check-in timestamp** — when the app last verified with our license server
- **No personal files or data are ever collected** — VUC compresses and decompresses locally; your files never leave your device

### 1.2 Newsletter Subscribers
- **Email address** (provided voluntarily through the website form)
- **Name** (optional, if provided)
- Used solely to send VUC updates, benchmarks, and enterprise offers. Never shared, sold, or spammed. One-click unsubscribe.

### 1.3 Enterprise License Holders
- **Company name** (required for license generation)
- **Contact email** (for support and renewal)
- **Hardware ID** (for license binding)
- **License key** (stored locally, verified against our server)

## 2. What We NEVER Collect

- **Your files or file contents** — VUC operates entirely on-device. Compressed files are never uploaded to any server.
- **Your browsing history**
- **Your location (GPS or IP-based)**
- **Your credentials or passwords**
- **Any third-party tracking data**

---

## 3. How We Use Data

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Hardware ID | License binding and anti-piracy verification | While license is active |
| Check-in timestamps | Offline grace period enforcement | Last 72 hours only |
| Email (newsletter) | Sending updates and offers | Until unsubscribed |
| Enterprise info | License generation and support | Duration of license + 12 months |

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- License state is stored **encrypted on your device** using XOR with a hardware-derived key
- Server-side data (check-ins, license records) is stored on encrypted GitHub servers
- Newsletter mail is managed through a GDPR-compliant provider
- **No credit card or payment data is ever stored by VUC** — enterprise payments are handled offline via invoice

VUC does not integrate with, sell data to, or share data with any third-party services except:

- **Email provider** — for newsletter delivery (email address only)
