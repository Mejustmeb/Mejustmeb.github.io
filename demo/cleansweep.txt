CleanSweep — find and clear duplicates & clutter, safely
=======================================================
A real tool by Echo. Scans a folder, finds duplicate files and clutter, and
clears them — with a preview and undo so nothing important is lost.

INSTALL: macOS .app, Linux .sh, or Windows .zip installer.

USAGE:
  cleansweep ~/Documents       scan a folder for duplicates + clutter
  cleansweep --dry-run         show what would be removed, remove nothing
  cleansweep --undo            restore the last clear
  cleansweep --help            full options

This demo text ships with the full tool — the installer is the real deliverable.
