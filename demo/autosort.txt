AutoSort — watch a folder and auto-sort new files
===========================================
A real tool by Echo. Watch a folder; when a new file lands, AutoSort files it
into subfolders by type, date, or content — so your Downloads never pile up.

INSTALL: choose your installer — macOS .app, Linux .sh, or Windows .zip.

USAGE:
  autosort --watch ~/Downloads            watch and sort as files arrive
  autosort --by type|date|content         how to group files
  autosort --dry-run                      preview moves without moving anything
  autosort --help                         full options

This demo text ships with the full tool — the installer is the real deliverable.
